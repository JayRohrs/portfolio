Services

I help small businesses, churches, nonprofits and community organisations with two kinds of work: the technology that keeps an office running, and the audio and video that gets a message out. They are separate services, so pick the one you need. Or start with a conversation and I will point you the right way.

The two services

IT & Systems

IT & Systems for small business

Microsoft 365, everyday support and practical automation, explained in plain words.

Small businesses, offices, nonprofits, and restaurant or accounting groups that do not have an in-house IT person, or whose IT person is stretched thin. I spent years as the main IT and systems resource for a restaurant-accounting firm, so I am used to explaining technical problems to people who just want their work to go smoothly.

  • Microsoft 365 setup and administration
  • Email and sign-in security
  • Everyday support and equipment

See IT & Systems services

AV & Production

AV & Media Production

Livestreams, live events and video that tells a clear story.

Churches, nonprofits, community groups and businesses that want an event streamed well, a story told on camera, or a video finished and published. I currently run livestreams and hybrid services for a Denver congregation, and I have made documentary, music and commercial video for more than twenty years.

  • Livestreams and hybrid events
  • Church and community AV
  • Field production and documentary

See AV & Production services

    I do the work and explain it in plain words.
