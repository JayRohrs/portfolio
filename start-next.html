After you send your request

This page is a short guide. It cannot see your email program, so it cannot tell you whether your message was sent.

  • If your email program opened

    Read the message, change anything you like, and press Send there. Afterwards, look in your Sent folder to check it went.

  • If nothing opened

    Go back to the form and choose “Copy request text”, then paste it into an email to jayrohrs@gmail.com. Some phones and computers have no email program set up, and very long requests can be too big for the link.

  • If you do not hear back

    Write to jayrohrs@gmail.com again, or send a message on LinkedIn. Messages can end up in spam folders on either side.

  • What happens next

    When we are in touch, we have a short conversation about what you need and what is getting in the way. If it is a fit, we agree one small, clearly scoped first step. The price is a quote on request.
