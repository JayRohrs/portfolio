IT & Systems for small business

Microsoft 365, everyday support and practical automation, explained in plain words.

Who it is for

Small businesses, offices, nonprofits, and restaurant or accounting groups that do not have an in-house IT person, or whose IT person is stretched thin. I spent years as the main IT and systems resource for a restaurant-accounting firm, so I am used to explaining technical problems to people who just want their work to go smoothly.

What I can help with

  • Microsoft 365 setup and administration

    User accounts, licences, permissions, Teams and SharePoint spaces, and the sign-in and access rules behind them.

  • Email and sign-in security

    Multi-factor sign-in rolled out across a team, and mail problems such as phishing and spam traced and sorted out.

  • Everyday support and equipment

    Remote help for people on Windows, Mac, iPhone and Android; choosing and setting up computers, printers and other office equipment; getting new people started and removing access when someone leaves; and small-office networks, domains and email hosting.

  • Restaurant and accounting-system checks

    Running and reviewing routine automation checks in restaurant accounting software such as Restaurant365, and working with a programmer when something needs correcting.

  • Documentation and handover

    Plain, step-by-step write-ups of how your systems work, and training so the next person can pick them up.

A first conversation, then a small first step

Tell me what is slowing you down. We talk it through, and if it is a fit we pick one small job to start with, such as a tidy-up of user accounts, a sign-in security check, or a written guide to a system only one person understands.
