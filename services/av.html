AV & Media Production

Livestreams, live events and video that tells a clear story.

Request AV & Production help

Who it is for

Churches, nonprofits, community groups and businesses that want an event streamed well, a story told on camera, or a video finished and published. I currently run livestreams and hybrid services for a Denver congregation, and I have made documentary, music and commercial video for more than twenty years.

What I can help with

  • Livestreams and hybrid events

    Multi-camera coverage for people in the room and people online: camera switching, graphics, remote participants and quick fixes when something goes wrong, using tools such as Wirecast, ProPresenter and Zoom.

  • Church and community AV

    Services and community events with PTZ cameras, sound and displays working together, plus written notes and debriefs so the setup keeps getting smoother.

  • Field production and documentary

    Camera and audio on location, interviews, and stories told over time, from a single shoot to a 20-episode series.

  • Editing and delivery

    Editing in Premiere Pro or Final Cut Pro, and publishing to YouTube.

  • Music, performance and short commercials

    Live performance video and short commercials, from concept and casting through lighting, filming and editing.

  • Edit one short video

    Finish and publish a video you already have footage for.

  • Film one interview

    Camera and audio on location for a single interview or short story.

  • Cover one event, with notes afterwards

    Written notes and a debrief after the event, so the setup keeps getting smoother.
