What a small first step can look like

These show the size of job I mean by a small first step. Yours may look different: we agree it together after the first conversation.

    Livestream a single event, and leave a plain written guide so the next person can run it.

  • Put a how-to on paper and on video

How it works

  1. Talk

    We have a short conversation about what you need and what is getting in the way.

  2. A small first step

    We agree one small, clearly scoped piece of work to start with, so you can see how it goes.

  3. Deliver

    I do the work and explain it in plain words.

  4. Follow up

    I check back, fix what needs fixing, and we decide together what comes next.
